# Literature review and previous studies behind the project

Prepared **27 October 2026, Asia/Riyadh**. Project status is grounded in the latest retained evidence through **25 October 2026 UTC**. This is a focused, explanatory literature review of the project's research lineage, not an exhaustive systematic review, a new experiment, or a claim to cover every EEG foundation model published by this date.

## 1. The research question in plain language

Motor-imagery brain-computer interfaces attempt to distinguish imagined actions from EEG. This project asks whether a pretrained EEG representation can adapt to a new person using very few labeled calibration examples, and whether that adaptation offers useful advantages over smaller neural networks and established statistical methods. The key comparison is not simply “large model versus small model”: it is the benefit of adaptation under controlled information, preprocessing, selection, and compute budgets.

The wider research plan studies adaptation, checkpoint exposure, causal decoding, and electrode reduction. The currently adopted first evaluation scope is narrower: **Cho2017 subjects 02–52, four methods, five seeds, five shots per class, and 1,020 retained evaluation cells**. All 52 subjects remain available to source selection under the outer-target exclusion rules. This is a retrospective scope amendment, not the original 52-target proposal. As of the latest accepted engineering checkpoint, **0/1,020 scientific evaluation cells are accepted**; software qualification must not be mistaken for a completed comparative study. See the [scope adoption](../phase2/cho_51_target_amendment_adoption_20261013.md), [research plan](../phase2_v2/BCI_Phase2_Research_v2.md), and [current handoff](../../HANDOFF.md).

### How this review was prepared

The existing reference reviews were cross-read against primary publications, author repositories, official dataset descriptions, and publication metadata on 27 October. BrowserOS Neo was not connected; the available research search/open tool supplied the public-source checks, without another browser UI, local model execution, or dataset downloads. Some publisher/PMC pages were blocked or timed out; those limits are identified below rather than presented as full-text verification. No author was contacted. Public checks do not refresh local scientific evidence or authenticate a checkpoint's training cohort.

## 3. What the datasets allow us to ask

Cho2017 is the primary setting because its cross-person cohort suits the calibration question. The local protocol uses left/right-hand imagery, not a four-class feet/rest task. Crucially, the loader's available within-class order is not authenticated acquisition chronology. “Earliest support” means earliest in that retained ordering, not proven earliest in a continuous session. This limitation matters even if all array hashes and split checks pass. See the [methodology and dataset audit summary](../methodology_draft.md).

BCI IV-2a supplies a smaller, explicitly four-class setting and two-session structure. Its original competition description also requires causal outputs: a decision cannot use future samples. This makes it useful both for development and for understanding why offline filtering results do not demonstrate deployable causal decoding. [Original protocol](https://www.bbci.de/competition/iv/desc_2a.pdf).

MMIDB must be interpreted by run, because identical event codes represent different actions in different runs. The project uses a defined imagery subset rather than treating movement, imagery, and rest as interchangeable labels. The dataset resource was published in 2009; Schalk et al.'s **BCI2000: A General-Purpose Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) System** is the related 2004 instrumentation publication, not an alternative dataset release date. [Official dataset documentation](https://physionet.org/content/eegmmidb/1.0.0/).

Neither the public cohort sizes nor the locally audited preprocessing views are additional independent participants. Profile A and Profile B are different processing views of existing recordings. A successful reconstruction proves a declared transformation, not missing chronology, clinical validity, or independence from pretraining.

## 4. Why the classical and small-network studies matter

The classical lineage provides meaningful alternatives to foundation-model adaptation. FBCSP asks whether carefully selected spatial-frequency structure already solves much of the task. Riemannian methods use covariance structure rather than a large learned representation. EA targets cross-person distribution mismatch before classification. Together these motivate evaluating whether a complex method adds value beyond appropriate signal representation and alignment. [Ang et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2012.00039), [Barachant et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22010143/), [He and Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05464).

EEGNet and the shallow/deep convolutional families provide smaller trainable comparators. Their role is not to create a deliberately weak baseline. Each needs a defensible information budget and selection opportunity. The local five-epoch Phase I neural sweep must not be compared as though it reproduced every published training schedule. [EEGNet paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08024), [Schirrmeister et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.05051).

The project's [EEGNet implementation review](../eegnet_implementation_review.md) documents differences in padding, dropout, and the classifier between the frozen Braindecode version and the authors' Keras implementation. The separate [MOABB reproduction investigation](../moabb_reference_reproduction.md) follows a linked Keras procedure with its own preprocessing, folds, callbacks, and environment. These are important scientific distinctions: an architecture name alone does not identify an experiment.

The owner accepted Gate A on 12 October under an **exact retrospective amendment**, accepting the historically audited linked procedure and negative controls as a usable reference baseline. The stronger original numerical reproduction claim remains unestablished. That acceptance does not transform all other EEGNet results into exact reproductions or sign the final experiment freeze. [Current Gate A decision](../phase2/gate_a_acceptance_20261012.md).

## 5. LaBraM: the pretrained representation and its provenance problem

LaBraM's core idea is to make heterogeneous EEG usable for shared pretraining: channel-time patches, spatial/temporal embeddings, a vector-quantized neural tokenizer, and prediction of masked neural codes. The paper reports transfer across several EEG tasks. Its partial-fine-tuning experiments motivate comparing frozen features and different adaptation depths rather than assuming full fine-tuning must win. These published results do not answer this project's controlled five-shot motor-imagery question. [LaBraM paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.18765v1).

There are two separate local questions:

1. **Implementation correspondence:** do the retained tensors, channel identities, patch layout, feature extraction, and restore path behave as declared?
2. **Training exposure:** which actual recordings and people contributed to the exact released checkpoint?

The first can be addressed with engineering checks; it does not answer the second. The project's [pretraining provenance review](../checkpoint_pretraining_provenance_20261010.md) and [dated public-source refresh](../checkpoint_public_provenance_refresh_20261011.md) record that MMIDB appears in the paper's pretraining inventory, while actual recording-level exposure of the checkpoint remains unresolved. Cho2017 and IV-2a being absent from a printed list is not exclusion proof. Likewise, a generic stored training path is not an authenticated cohort manifest.

The E1 proposal therefore remains observational: cross-dataset masked-prediction differences could reflect acquisition, task, noise, or preprocessing differences, not memorization. A compatible tokenizer and complete masked-prediction route must first be verified; substituting a different objective would answer a different question. The provenance-aware restoration factory improves traceability and controlled construction. It cannot manufacture missing external training history.

## 6. Parameter-efficient adaptation: what transfers, and what does not

**LoRA** learns a low-rank residual update while preserving base weights. The mechanism offers a principled way to constrain adaptation and reduce trainable parameters; whether it improves EEG calibration is an empirical question. The local reference notes document initialization differences and an unmerged implementation, so the original paper's no-added-inference-latency argument must not be copied as a local measured result. [Hu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685), [local adaptation review](../adaptation_reference_notes.md).

**Bottleneck adapters** introduce small residual modules. The local wrapper's placement and zero-up-projection identity differ from the complete Transformer recipe reviewed from Houlsby et al. It is legitimate to evaluate that declared variant, but not to call every adapter wrapper an exact reproduction. [Houlsby et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/houlsby19a.html).

**Prompt and prefix tuning** condition a frozen model through learned continuous representations. Lester et al. study input soft prompts, while Li and Liang study continuous prefixes for generation. The project's support-conditioned prompt generator adds another distinction: prompts depend on a person's support examples rather than simply being one optimized task prefix. Sequence-token insertion and per-layer key/value prefixes also need different implementation descriptions. Findings in language generation or T5 scaling do not establish EEG scaling laws. [Lester et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.243/), [Li and Liang](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.353/).

These studies motivate the wider seven-system research roster: six LaBraM adaptation variants and separate TCPL, plus EEGNet and EA-tangent reference anchors. They do **not** expand the currently adopted four-method first tranche, which is LaBraM linear, LoRA, full fine-tuning, and EEGNet. Extra methods require their own declared scope and feasible execution plan.

## 7. TCPL: relevant comparator, unresolved reproduction

Wang et al. published TCPL on 24 November 2025. The method builds support-conditioned prompts for a meta-trained TCN/Transformer, with target inference using a fixed backbone. It is a separate system comparator, not a way to isolate prompting on the same LaBraM architecture. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2025.1689286).

The retained [reference review](../phase2/tcpl_reference_review.md) identifies specific unresolved issues: the article describes four Cho imagery classes despite the original two-hand dataset; BCI LOSO wording conflicts with a blanket ten-fold description; normalization fit scope and constructor details need clarification. Ambiguity is not proof of query leakage or misconduct. The current public excerpts still expose the class/protocol discrepancies. No published mean should become a local acceptance target before the protocol is resolved.

Later local TCN, prompt, model, episode, and source-selection components exist under explicit local contracts; they do not retroactively verify author architecture, checkpoints, or numerical parity. The early label-conditioned prototype is another local variant, not faithful TCPL. Published findings and local reconstruction must remain separately labeled.

## 8. Evaluation lessons that shape this project

### Separate adaptation from evaluation

Cawley and Talbot show why choosing hyperparameters on a noisy evaluation criterion can produce optimistic results. Here that motivates source-subject inner selection and a genuinely held-out target-query set. Target support may supply the expressly permitted calibration information, but target-query scores must not choose ranks, depths, epochs, thresholds, or a winning method later reported as prespecified. [Primary study](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html).

The local research contract also proposes nested support sizes with a common query set, support-only fitting of target alignment/normalization, and fixed query-time state. Fairness means matched permitted information and credible tuning opportunities, not mechanically identical optimization steps for every architecture. A no-target-gradient support-conditioned system must not be forced into a different method merely to equalize step counts.

### Pair people, not seed labels

The declared unit for cross-subject inference is the person. Seeds are averaged within person before paired differences are analyzed. Deterministic result copies are not independent fits, and overlapping windows are not extra independent participants. Shared subject/support/query identities are prerequisites to meaningful paired comparisons. These are local design commitments in the [statistical procedure](../statistical_procedure.md), not claims that all existing records establish universal independence.

### Distinguish superiority, uncertainty, and equivalence

Lakens explains why failure to find a difference is not evidence that two methods are equivalent. Formal equivalence requires justified bounds and the specified testing procedure; bounds derived from feasibility do not automatically express clinical interchangeability. [Equivalence primer](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550617697177).

The older research plan discusses paired TOST and a frozen margin of ±0.0520290793 balanced-accuracy units. The **current adopted Cho amendment instead retains the specified exploratory 90% interval and does not introduce a formal equivalence decision**. Neither this review nor a software test changes that decision. The first-tranche primary contrast remains LoRA minus linear probe at five shots after averaging the five declared seeds within each of the 51 target subjects. [Current scope/analysis adoption](../phase2/cho_51_target_amendment_adoption_20261013.md).

### Measure a usable interface, not only an offline classifier

Thompson et al. emphasize performance measures appropriate to the BCI task. This supports the project's separation of classification quality from command timing, error activation, and abstention. Processing a ready window quickly does not remove the time required to acquire that window; selective accuracy must be reported alongside coverage. [Performance tutorial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4185283/).

Locally, saved epochs were filtered offline. A causal ring buffer or causal TCN does not make that earlier preprocessing causal. The retained Windows/LSL demonstration checked transport, display, gating, and neutral decisions; all-abstention integration is not useful live-brain control. Future E3 work needs a causal signal path, state-reset/delay checks, batch-one end-to-end timing, and suitable idle data before false-activation claims. Similarly, E4 must distinguish zeroing channels in a full-montage system from training and deployment using only retained electrodes. [Real-time evidence and limits](../realtime_system.md), [E3/E4 research contract](../phase2_v2/BCI_Phase2_Research_v2.md).

## 9. The gap this project can defensibly address

The synthesis above motivates a **controlled, provenance-aware comparison of pretrained adaptation under low-label cross-person constraints**. It does not establish that nobody has studied few-shot EEG adaptation, that LoRA is novel, or that this project's method is already superior. A defensible contribution would be the combination of explicit data/checkpoint identities, source-only selection, matched target partitions, transparent failures, measured resource cost, paired uncertainty, and appropriately limited deployment claims.

The latest progress is primarily the engineering foundation for such evidence. The separately versioned factory and numerical-worker integration were qualified within bounded engineering scope. The canonical metadata boundary passed 133 complementary cases across three guarded batches and 34 distinct controls repeated across those batches. Those achievements verify their declared contracts, not a 1,020-cell scientific result, full canonical execution admission, or the wider research program. See the [numerical integration qualification](../phase2/cho51_provenance_numerical_worker_qualification_20261025.md) and [metadata boundary qualification](../phase2/cho51_canonical_boundary_qualification_20261025.md).

The remaining scientific dependencies include authenticated checkpoint-cohort evidence and the unsigned final experiment freeze. The stronger original Gate A claim remains unestablished despite the owner's narrow retrospective acceptance. Subjects 02–52/source cohort 52 remain the adopted scope; the original subject01 attempt budget is exhausted and is not reset by this review. New adapter design or validation requires a separately reviewed scope and fresh coordination; old grants and launchers are inert.

**Bottom line:** previous studies make the research question credible and supply strong comparators. This repository has substantial audited implementation and historical baseline evidence, but it has not yet supplied accepted results for its current primary scientific comparison. The next contribution must come from an admitted, frozen, traceable evaluation—not from reinterpreting engineering success as predictive performance.
