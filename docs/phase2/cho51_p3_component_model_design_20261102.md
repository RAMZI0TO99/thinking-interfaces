## Independent review and preservation

The [v1 review](../../runs/coordination/cho51_p3_component_model_design_20261102_v1/design-review-v1.json)
identified CM-D1: start/end records could meet the explicit checks without any
primary PASS, and the trace grammar lacked required frame cardinality. The
accepted revision requires exactly one primary PASS after each successful case's
full lifecycle and before its sole CASE_END, with sticky negative outcomes and
explicit failed-fixture/not-started conservation. Negative events bind exact
contiguous TRACE counts, owners, kinds and frame ordinals. Empty start/end,
duplicate/early PASS and missing/extra trace transcripts are planned negative
subcases within the unchanged32 method identities.

and [Root adoption](../../runs/coordination/cho51_p3_component_model_design_20261102_v1/root-design-acceptance.json)
