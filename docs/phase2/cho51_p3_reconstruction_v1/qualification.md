These are design obligations, not implemented or executed tests. Preserve all
456 existing distinct methods and their exact cohorts/controls until a reviewed
successor maps them explicitly. New producer methods must have named identities,
scenario mapping and exact aggregate consumers before Q. Avoid a nominal pass
count that omits an inherited method or treats a duplicate sharing run as new.

Small Q fixtures qualify mechanisms; they do not exercise all74/53 real pages,
fresh native custody, complete real-source reconciliation, or the real-case
output/time fit. RP01/RP03/RP05/RP06/RP08/RP24 therefore also require actual P
evidence for their real-input claims. The implementation map must mark which
parts remain unexercised and distinguish source inspection, Q fixture execution
and accepted actual evidence. One scenario may need multiple methods/controls;
24 design rows are not a promised24-test implementation.

No test plan here reclassifies the existing117 candidate cases,34 V controls,
26 original qualification scenarios, old48-case custody matrix or pending L
qualification. FP01-FP12 remain unexecuted producer obligations. This design
must not be used to stamp them complete. No runtime operation is admitted by
acceptance of these documents or the accompanying static capacity observation.
