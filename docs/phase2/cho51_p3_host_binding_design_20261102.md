The [binding contract](cho51_p3_host_binding_v1/binding.md) specifies bounded
frames, original session/operation/source joins, exact native integer transport,
consumed-failure transitions and owned cancellation/closure. A supplied-frame
parser returns unauthenticated data; it cannot mint a provider witness. A fake
host, Root-authored JSON, source-file hash or PID alone supplies no authenticity.

The production interface is deliberately unavailable until the actual provider
API, evidence schemas, original identity/delegation, durable claim behavior,
complete artifact domain and resource/accounting behavior are established. A
new provider database, lock, cache or audit export is not an exempt artifact.
Original platform messages can be provenance inputs only when their authentic
identity, persistence and accounting are actually supported.

## Material compatibility and feasibility findings

SC1's exact census contains only its16 metadata paths. Q also has fixture,
supervisor and caller partitions. HB1 proposes an immutable typed parent join
and complete parent census before deriving a metadata view. Assigning another
UUID or discarding other outputs would not establish conservation. The proposed
mapping needs a successor; the frozen SC1 authority/ledger is unchanged.

Host methods are synchronous. Checkpoints surrounding a call cannot stop it from
blocking. The proposed200ms per-call ceiling requires real bounded native
cancellation and original-channel closure within the unchanged overall guards;
it is not a measured latency guarantee. Failure bookkeeping after a guard refusal
may use only its remaining original cleanup allowance, with UNKNOWN retained.

Postpublication confirmations and actual reviews cannot be fabricated ahead of
their evidence. The frozen publisher has no qualified durable handoff across
asynchronous review waits. A finite production lifecycle must account for all
work and closure without resetting a clock, resuming a failed transaction or
borrowing the unrelated C export-stage allowance. HB1 keeps that prerequisite
explicit rather than assuming a peer will answer within a runtime window.

The [readiness record](cho51_p3_host_binding_v1/readiness.json) also records a
capture counterexample:1,048,576 permitted control-character text bytes can
require6,291,456 JSON string bytes before headers, exceeding the unchanged1MiB
raw-capture slot. The stream ceiling alone cannot establish complete capture.
This is not proof that every finite Q output is too large. The exact producer
grammar and real tool transport must bound every success/failure envelope, or
admission refuses. No stream cap is reduced and no evidence is truncated.

Runtime readiness is REFUSED_PENDING_PREREQUISITES. Complete-Q timing/output
feasibility remains NOT_ESTABLISHED. The old prefix observation is not a bound
on changed full-Q startup, suites, controls, host calls or final verification.

## Qualification and review scope

The [qualification plan](cho51_p3_host_binding_v1/qualification.md) separates
supplied-model checks from real provider/native evidence. The exact existing
[roster](cho51_p3_host_binding_v1/roster.json) has580 distinct identities:
516 held legacy methods,10 QD and54 SC1. New HB implementation methods require
their own exact frozen identities;24 HB scenarios are not24 passing tests.
All40 SC and36 diagnostic obligations remain unqualified.

Future native units must finish every linked subcase and required guard/cleanup
control within one original bounded operation. Their component-only outcomes
cannot be aggregated into complete Q. Full probe32, then separate100, then384
still require all eight modes, every cohort control, final verification,
recensus, publication and owned closure under each original clock.

The proposed native-fixture profile reserves a complete16-slot inner ledger
plus at most16 auxiliary files, at most32 files/4MiB inside the existing fixture
partition. It needs a reviewed fixed fixture namespace and parent join; it is
not permission to monkeypatch SC1's production path. Every existing global and
partition cap, time/RAM/disk/stop/freshness/thread/device guard and custody rule
remains. No old launcher, failed UUID or admission is reused.

Independent [design review](../../runs/coordination/cho51_p3_host_binding_design_20261102_v1/design-review-v2.json)
and [Root adoption](../../runs/coordination/cho51_p3_host_binding_design_20261102_v1/root-design-acceptance.json)
bind the accepted scope and exact published-byte confirmations. Root is the sole phase-record publisher;
the reviewer sends a message and confirms the exact transcribed bytes. A
different reviewer checks final metadata/documentation closeout. Neither review
is runtime qualification or actual-case acceptance.

The first review identified two design gaps. Their corrections are accepted in

after re-review; the four affected v1 design files remain byte-exact in the draft
archive.
