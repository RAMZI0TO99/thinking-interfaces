# Scope decision and obligation conservation

## Decision boundary

The owner requests design and review with all guards and obligations preserved
and P disabled. Within that scope, Root selects a bounded observation artifact
for the next engineering implementation. This is a new, explicitly narrower
evidence product, not an amendment to the requirements of complete reconciliation,
the kernel, P, G2, the 117 candidates or the scientific study.

| Alternative | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete resolver continuation in the existing kernel | Refused | At least 31,002 rows before additional overhead exceeds 24,000. |
| Combine both historical inventories in one state | Refused for that combined layout | 28,892 distinct nodes alone exceed 24,000. |
| Compress, deduplicate, page or shard the same continued state | No escape adopted | Full historical multiplicity and every intermediate/canonical charge still apply. |
| Raise limits, prune history, omit debt, revise accounting or reset across operations | Not adopted | Conflicts with this task's preservation requirements. |
| Fixed non-resumable source-observation probe | Selected design scope | A finite useful comparison can fit a separately explicit bounded report without claiming a continued or complete reconciled state. |
| Complete reconciliation/P/L/G2 through accumulating probes | Refused | No aggregate consumer, debt-discharge rule or reconstruction acceptance exists; positive local observations do not satisfy the global contract. |

The probe cannot be advertised as a solution making the full continuation
feasible. If the requested deliverable is a complete continued state, the answer
under unchanged guards remains refusal. A later change to that deliverable or
its guards would need its own explicit scope/contract decision and authorization.
This design makes no such future change on the owner's behalf.

## Conservation

Let U_R be the exact ordered 18,793 resolver row identities
(index pin, page ordinal, row ordinal), with full row payloads. Let U_I be the
separate exact 13,406 inventory occurrences; repeated ordinal uses are distinct.
The frozen bundles remain complete and unchanged. Embedded binding/effect
records remain in their owning payloads and are not reinterpreted as extra
distinct AST nodes. Historical indices, descriptors and exact bytes retain
their original provenance, scope and custody limitations.

For this probe S consists of resolver page54, zero-based rows176 through213.
The observed set is always S, never a replacement U_R. The complement is the
set difference U_R minus S, not a newly enumerated reset state. Its count is
18,755. All 13,406 U_I entries are outside this probe. Even S remains unresolved:
positive structural comparison discharges zero source, binding, effect, ancestry,
capability, custody or historical obligations. Within S the ten recorded effects
and one unresolved binding load are retained, not approved.

The enclosing raw page retains all 256 rows including 218 outside S. The whole
raw index is also retained. Their presence supplies exact contextual bytes; it
does not certify unsupplied pages, the global index's semantic closure, or other
sources. The untouched original bundle remains the authoritative historical
store. The small probe is expressly not its replacement and makes no
exhaustive-ledger claim. Consequently an index reference to the rest of the
bundle is not used as a payload substitute for an accepted checkpoint/report.

Every probe result repeats `obligations_discharged: 0` and
`global_ledger_mutated: false`. No operation emits a cursor, predecessor,
sequence, frame, graph, resume token or transition state. A future consumer must
reject this schema wherever a kernel state, lossless bundle, reconciliation
report, plan, admission or P aggregate is required. Repeating or selecting
another probe requires a separately reviewed scope; no automatic loop over
modules/pages or stitching service is included.

Original kernel equations and full-chain guards remain exactly as in
[capacity.md](../cho51_p3_lossless_state_v1/capacity.md). They are neither applied
to an invented zero graph nor recalculated using selected records to claim a fit.
Any request for a checkpoint or continuation routes to `REFUSED_SCOPE` before
construction. Obligations outside a non-authorizing observation do not become
uncounted state because no accepted obligation-bearing state is produced.

## Unchanged authority

All G2, launch, capability, scientific, historical-custody, current-P-custody and
coverage grants remain false. Old `131bc595` outer/bootstrap UNKNOWN, Bootstrap004
consumed, diagnostics005 held, custody UNRESOLVED and006 unadmitted persist.
The complete reconstruction contract, seed/root-requester provenance, exact
occurrence joins, native origins, transitive imports and host capability debts
remain pending. Historical observations and source hash equality cannot
authenticate a current runtime, native handle or process generation.
