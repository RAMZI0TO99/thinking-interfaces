# Original operation, effects and finite acceptance boundary

This design targets a future S140 qualification operation. Q360 regression is
a separate fixed-profile operation with its own complete controls, unused
attempt, source/runtime pins, clocks and actual review. They are not Q500, and
neither can be partitioned to evade a required single-operation limit. This
document admits neither operation and does not replay a historical launcher.

## Exact scope before any effect

The full source census is1062 identities in49 modules. Q360 is unchanged;
S140 is exactly the frozen60 CM6 plus80 CM6i methods and is disjoint from Q.
All702 held rows remain:516 BASELINE_HELD and186 NEW_TEST_UNQUALIFIED. S140 is
still held. The46 CM6S implementation methods are outside both fixed cohorts;
running S140 would not qualify those46 methods or all of the new wrapper.
They need a separately reviewed qualification/cohort decision before operational
reliance. Source review alone cannot close that bootstrap obligation.

Full original discovery must account for all1062 rows and49 module pins, including
held methods, before selection. S bodies' frozen fixtures independently construct
historical supplied contexts, including CM6i C1016/45. Do not rebase those fixtures
to outer C1062/49 or treat their supplied inner results as original Q execution.
An intentionally negative/refused inner fixture can satisfy an outer assertion;
its result is not automatically an outer S failure. Inner and outer operation,
context, ordinal, byte and counter domains must remain separate at every join.
The original516 held bodies outside the546-body index remain open; discovery
effects of their modules are still in scope even when their bodies are not run.

An eventual independently adopted operation manifest must name the exact profile,
ordered methods, complete source/runtime/code/type manifests, all required
qualification/control cases, dependencies, native capabilities and paths/owners.
Its control population cannot be inferred from the706 normal S event count or
the32 new scenarios in this package. Existing local117/V34/26, CM6-32, S140-24,
HB/SC/QD/native and all other prior obligations retain their individual scopes.
Unknown membership or coverage is a pre-effect refusal, not permission to run
only an easier subset. No current original manifest is fabricated here.

Q/S and independent source/runtime/producer manifests precede D; D precedes E;
E precedes K_S, original collection, events, terminal and join. source_set excludes
D/E and every dependent report/admission/review artifact. No source embeds its
own pin. Supplied hash shape/equality cannot prove dependency closure, provenance,
or authentic predecessor/current adoption. Original expectations must come from
the independent accepted source boundary, not observed reports or Facts.

bounded supervisor relationship and one sequential test child. An observer is
a role in that reviewed topology, not an extra worker/service. Every compiler,
helper, thread, handle, pipe and process requires explicit ownership and budget.
No process is added by this design.

manifest cannot solve this by pinning its own later hash. A role's permission
to publish does not prove that role has already terminated.

support the operation namespace; do not patch or bypass frozen validators.

## Complete effect and failure mapping

The [effect ledger](effects.json) preserves all16 CM5 boundary classes and
references all546 indexed bodies. It does not call a regex source index a
recursive effect proof. Every reachable operation, import/initializer, fixture,
formatter, callback, native call, allocation and cleanup path requires a row
with exact source/entry/type/input domain, maximum cardinality/recursion,
intermediate bytes, output/file/handle effects, live interval, owner and failure
transition. An unknown bound or unjoined source row blocks W0.

Normal, expected-negative, producer refusal, secondary refusal-helper failure,
resource/stop/timeout, native/transport failure, cancellation and failed cleanup
are different routes. Before effects, missing prerequisites return
REFUSED_UNADMITTED. After effects or consumption may have occurred, failures
consume that original attempt and follow only reviewed owned cleanup; they do
not restart T0, resume a prefix, choose a fresh UUID or free an old attempt.

Complete supplied negative events and a complete refused terminal are eligible
only for a negative supplied join. All-PASS events with refused terminal remain
negative. An incomplete trace/footer/counter/detachment/EOF/custody route cannot
be converted to a complete negative join merely to fit the schema. Preserve the
available raw prefix, including empty, in the bounded original failure path.
The exact original failure envelope and packing remain an open gate.

Reference unittest routes retain their differences: decorated early skips and
KeyboardInterrupt can miss required duration/cleanup; nested subtests/failfast
may miss a flat result; late fixture exceptions can follow all case PASS events.
If reachable without a qualified faithful mapping, refuse before admission.
Do not flatten, synthesize, silently repair mutable globals or suppress failure
work. A nonformatting result sink does not remove upstream repr/str/pprint/difflib,
mock diagnostics, parser/native errors, destructor or traceback effects.

Native enforcement must remain outside tested mutable os/shutil/ctypes bindings,
mocks and callbacks. Source-bound restoration checks complement continuous
enforcement; they cannot prove that mutations never existed during a case.
No ordinary Python check, thread or fixed model fault enum proves termination
of a blocked/native call or bounds actual adversarial ingress.

## Allocation and clock decision

The [allocation ledger](allocations.json) carries the existing33 report slots,
their owners and capacities. It is a reservation proposal, not a native parent
map or permission to write. Q/S/D/E are independently pinned source inputs;
their opens/rechecks and every future staged/copy/temp byte are charged. A
read-only source input is not a free output slot or outside-clock preparation.

Exact retained raw stdout/stderr remain in their existing slots. A possible
successor may return bounded references rather than inline escaped streams,
but that is not adopted as a complete capture solution here: original stream
order, externally observed merge order, chunk bounds, raw tool return, EOF/error,
cancellation and custody still require exact bounded witness packing into
existing roles. No truncation or untracked sidecar may establish success.

T0 precedes original bootstrap effects and never resets. Preserve whole90,
work60, cleanup5, hard65, child55, suite40, native12, fault2 and startup5 seconds.
Child completion is bounded by min(T0+55,T0+60-F-M), with independently justified
remaining final work F and margin M. Each launch/case also requires enough time
for its worst-case route and those reserves. Cleanup deadline is no later than
the original fault+5 and original hard bounds. W8/W9 must have explicit costs
under the applicable original work/hard/whole clocks; no assumed free gap,
human-review pause, retained compilation or another window supplies headroom.

## Acyclic prerequisite-qualification admission

W0 above is the full S140/Q360 admission predicate. It is not the admission for
an experiment whose purpose is to qualify a prerequisite. A prerequisite may
have an unqualified functional outcome, but its driving, enforcement, effects,
capture, observation and cleanup basis cannot depend on that outcome or on the
candidate acting as its own authority. The following directed dependency path
is mandatory; it is an obligation graph, not an assertion of available drivers.

| Node | Independently established basis before it can be used | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B0: original trust/guard/observation root | Original host/OS authority, identity, stop/clock, containment, resource, capture/EOF and owned-cleanup observation accepted independently of the candidate and any supplied Facts |UNIMPLEMENTED / UNQUALIFIED / NOT_ESTABLISHED |
| B1: bounded prerequisite driver | Exact reviewed driver/runtime and finite input/effect/failure/allocation plan; B0 supplies its actual enforcement and observation, including driver failure |UNIMPLEMENTED / UNQUALIFIED; no existing driver inferred |
| B2: prerequisite targets | Each native/provider/ingress/capture/wrapper target is driven by B1 under B0 and only already accepted lower-level dependencies; candidate output is compared with independent original witnesses |UNIMPLEMENTED / UNQUALIFIED; every needed target remains open |
| B3: complete fixed-profile runner | Accepted B0/B1/B2 evidence, complete original manifest/controls and all seven full-profile gates before W0 |REFUSED_UNADMITTED |

Edges may point only from a later node to an independently established earlier
basis. B0's trust basis must be selected and justified from original host/OS
authority under the project policy; it is not another node recursively certified
by this candidate. Establishing that basis is a separate bounded design and
acceptance obligation. If no independent root is available, work stops at source
design; no bootstrap run is inferred or authorized. Historical Q/P receipts,
the current assistant/tool session and supplied host tokens do not establish B0.

Before any prerequisite experiment, Root must record a distinct, independently
reviewed qualification admission: exact target claim and nonclaims, B0/B1 identity
and accepted evidence, acyclic dependency pins, unused attempt, finite command/
input/type/effect/failure domains, every original guard, exact output/ownership
map and cleanup/capture/observation route. The only permitted unknown is the
target's scoped functional result inside that already bounded domain. Unknown
guard effectiveness, effect reachability, failure resource bounds or custody
is a refusal before effects. An unqualified target cannot supply the clock,
stop, containment, capture or closure witness used to accept its own test.

Each actual result requires independent saved-evidence review before the next
dependency may use it. A failed/ambiguous experiment remains consumed and cannot
become a root. No circular borrowing of full-profile W0 acceptance, no target
dependency on a later B2 peer, and no qualification of B1 by candidate testimony
are allowed. The same topology and limits apply: this graph adds no service,
worker, output role or relaxed clock. Prerequisite records never combine into
a passing S140/Q360 run. The46 wrapper methods still need an explicit independent
qualification/cohort decision; source review or existing S membership cannot
silently provide it.

## Finite path to a reviewable original runner

1. Freeze an exact operation manifest and complete reachable effect/type domains,
   including the46 held CM6S methods' separate qualification obligation and all
   original controls. Resolve every unknown allocation/lifetime and file owner.
2. Design and review the B0/B1 trust and prerequisite-driver basis together with
   the original provider/ingress/capture witness contract and
   its exact packing into existing roles. Prove that source restoration, EOF,
   revocation, terminal publication, census and owned closeout are noncircular
   and finite on every declared failure route.
3. Implement separate versioned successors only after that design is accepted;
   preserve frozen sources and account every new method as held unless an
   independent cohort decision explicitly includes it. Source-review both code
   and the complete worst-case budget before any execution request.
4. Only after the independently established B0/B1 basis is accepted, qualify B2
   capabilities and failure routes through the separate bounded prerequisite
   admissions above and independently reviewed saved evidence. A prerequisite-specific
   qualification is its own scoped claim; it cannot aggregate fragments into a
   passing single S140 or Q360 operation or bypass whole-operation caps.
5. Only when every gate and complete-operation cost/reserve is established may
   Root consider a fresh original fixed-profile admission. Successful actual
   review may record only its exact scoped evidence; unrelated held debt and
   historical UNKNOWN custody remain.

The next source-only target is step2's **independent B0/B1 basis and original
provider/ingress/capture witness contract with fixed-slot packing**, with
step1's source/effect/allocation closure
remaining mandatory. No diagnostic, native build or runtime is authorized here.
